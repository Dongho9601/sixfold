Return to Sixfold

Rules, privacy, and credits

A free game

Sixfold is free and non-commercial. Gold is a game score with no monetary or material value. There are no advertisements, payments, donations, cash-out, or monetary or material prizes.

Each run starts with 100 Gold and lasts up to 100 rolls. Your browser draws outcomes and settles wagers. Only the final balance after roll 100 can enter your personal leaderboard; reaching zero Gold earlier ends the run without a score. Zero Gold after exactly 100 rolls is a valid score. The leaderboard keeps your best 10 eligible completed runs in this browser. Earlier peaks and the current board’s net income do not determine your final score.

Bets, payouts, and changing odds

The board has 1, 3, and 5 in the top row (Odds) and 2, 4, and 6 in the bottom row (Evens). Columns cover 1–2, 3–4, and 5–6. Red covers 1, 4, and 5; Black covers 2, 3, and 6. Bet whole amounts of at least 1 Gold, up to your bankroll in total. You may place multiple bets, and every bet covering the rolled number pays.

Numbers return ×6, columns ×3, and rows or colors ×2. Returns include the winning stake. For example, a single 10 Gold number bet that hits returns 60 Gold, for a net gain of 50 Gold. Your bankroll changes by the total return minus all stakes on that roll. Every roll clears the settled bets and uses one roll, including losses and break-even results.

Odds are hidden by default and need not be equal. Tilt describes their concentration in six stages: NEARLY EVEN, SLIGHT BIAS, MODERATE BIAS, STRONG BIAS, HEAVY BIAS, and EXTREME BIAS. A stronger bias means the odds are more concentrated on fewer numbers. “NEARLY EVEN” means the chances are similar; it does not promise an exactly fair die. Tilt does not reveal which numbers are favored. After an ordinary payout, the rolled number gives some of its probability above the 2% floor to the other five numbers. This happens once per paying roll, even if the combined bets lose Gold overall. A complete miss leaves the odds unchanged.

Board net, Shake, and Jackpot

BOARD NET shows cumulative net income on the current die: all returns minus all settled stakes since it arrived. A 10 Gold loss followed by a 50 Gold gain gives +40 Gold. Changes to the odds after ordinary payouts keep the same counter. A roll that gains or loses Gold unlocks a free Shake; a break-even roll does not unlock it, but preserves an already-earned Shake. Shake draws fresh odds, clears unrolled bets, and resets BOARD NET to zero. Your bankroll and remaining rolls stay the same. Shake locks again until another gain or loss.

Bet exclusively on the least likely single number and hit it to receive the ×24 Jackpot return instead of ×6. Adding more to that same number is allowed; any other positive bet removes Jackpot eligibility. Exact ties for the least likely number go to the lowest number. A 10 Gold Jackpot returns 240 Gold, a 230 Gold net gain. The gain appears before a fresh die arrives, resets BOARD NET, and locks Shake. That replacement uses no additional roll and preserves your earned Gold.

A playable tutorial guides your first visit directly on the game board, using the same betting cells, die, Roll dice and Shake controls. Its practice Gold, turns, and guided outcomes are separate from your saved game. You can skip it at any time. Open Help to replay the tutorial or retrieve How to play, including wager editing, keyboard controls, a probability-transfer example, and these rules in seven languages. Show probabilities starts off in every run. Turning it on requires confirmation and permanently excludes that run from personal records, even if you hide the probabilities later. Previous records stay. Starting a new run restores eligibility and hides the probabilities.

Personal saves in this browser

Sixfold automatically saves the settled run, board probabilities and starting bankroll for the BOARD NET counter, roll count, last result, record eligibility, nickname, sound and motion preferences, guide language and dismissal, which version of the tutorial you finished or skipped, recent bet amounts, and best 10 completed runs in localStorage under sixfold:save:v1. Practice progress is temporary and starts over on refresh. Settlement is saved before animation, so refreshing or reopening can continue an unfinished run with the same board net. Older saves without a board starting bankroll begin the counter at zero on first load, keeping their Gold, progress, and records. Last-second setting changes can use a temporary sixfold:settings:pending:v1 journal during navigation. Unrolled draft wagers clear on refresh. Restarting discards the current run and draft and starts a new 100 Gold run, preserving personal records and settings.

Names and scores remain in this browser. There are no accounts, session cookies, shared rankings, cloud synchronization, or game servers. Clearing Sixfold’s stored data or the browser’s site data removes saved progress. Saves do not transfer between browsers, devices, or origins. If storage is blocked or full, the game remains playable and explains that progress cannot be saved. An unreadable or incompatible save starts a fresh run, retaining the first unreadable save under sixfold:save:recovery when storage permits.

Static hosting and network data

The game loads static HTML, JavaScript, CSS, and its favicon. Gameplay makes no backend requests and includes no analytics or third-party asset requests. Choosing an external link, such as the creator’s website or a reference below, takes you to that website, which has its own data practices. The hosting provider may process ordinary page and asset requests; see GitHub’s privacy statement for hosting information.
