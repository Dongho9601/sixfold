Return to Sixfold

Rules, privacy, and credits

A free game

Sixfold is free and non-commercial. Gold is a game score with no monetary or material value. There are no advertisements, payments, donations, cash-out, or monetary or material prizes.

Each run starts with 100 Gold and lasts up to 100 rolls. Your browser draws outcomes and settles wagers. Only the final balance after roll 100 can enter your personal leaderboard; an earlier bankruptcy records no score. The leaderboard keeps your best 10 completed runs in this browser. The in-game How to play guide explains all targets, payouts, Tilt, Shake, Jackpot, and controls. Show probabilities is off at the start of every run. Turning it on requires confirmation and excludes that run’s score from personal records, even if it is turned off later. Previous records stay. Odds and game rules can be inspected in the browser.

Personal saves in this browser

Sixfold automatically saves the settled run, board probabilities, roll count, last result, record eligibility, nickname, sound and motion preferences, guide language and dismissal, recent bet amounts, and best 10 completed runs in localStorage under sixfold:save:v1. Settlement is saved before animation, so refreshing or reopening can continue an unfinished run. Last-second setting changes can use a temporary sixfold:settings:pending:v1 journal during navigation. Unrolled draft wagers clear on refresh. Restarting discards only the current run and draft, preserving personal records and settings.

Names and scores remain in this browser. There are no accounts, session cookies, shared rankings, cloud synchronization, or game servers. Clearing Sixfold’s stored data or the browser’s site data removes saved progress. Saves do not transfer between browsers, devices, or origins. If storage is blocked or full, the game remains playable and explains that progress cannot be saved. An unreadable or incompatible save starts a fresh run, retaining the first unreadable save under sixfold:save:recovery when storage permits.

Static hosting and network data

The game loads static HTML, JavaScript, CSS, and its favicon. Gameplay makes no backend requests. There are no analytics or third-party runtime requests. The hosting provider may process ordinary page and asset requests; see GitHub’s privacy statement for hosting information.

Credits

The bundled die, board, icons, and favicon are CSS or SVG. Sound is generated locally with Web Audio. Fonts come from your operating system. No external artwork, fonts, recordings, or reference screenshots are fetched by the game.

The repository credits the roulette table layout at Casino.org and Balatro screenshots on PlayStation as design references; their assets are not bundled. The development tools include Vite (MIT), tsx (MIT), TypeScript (Apache-2.0), and Playwright (Apache-2.0). Node.js is used only for development and tests. The bundled Vite helper’s MIT notice is included in the static output.
