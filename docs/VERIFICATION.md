## Architecture and persistence

`src/game/rules.ts` preserves the existing payout, probability, transfer, Tilt, and Jackpot formulas. `src/game/run.ts` supplies pure run transitions and exact personal-record ordering. `src/game/random.ts` uses Web Crypto on the former RNG’s uniform 48-bit grid. `src/save.ts` validates and stores a version-1 JSON save at `sixfold:save:v1`; Gold and scores remain exact decimal strings with `bigint` arithmetic.

Settled state, settings, records, and completion receipt persist together before animation starts. Tests cover refresh during roll and final-roll animations, completion deduplication through repeated reloads, early bankruptcy versus a valid final zero, probability-assisted exclusion, named restart, preserved settings and records, navigation preference journaling without overwriting a newer run, adjacent 81-digit scores, stable ties, stale run revisions, rapid inputs, and two same-origin tabs. Malformed/incompatible data recovers without touching neighboring project keys, and blocked/quota-limited storage leaves play and restart available with a save warning.

The browser UI retains the board, controls, input guards, die/replacement animations, rewards, procedural audio, and seven-language guide. The leaderboard displays this browser’s best 10 eligible completed runs. Tilt displays a smaller white Tilt N heading above the larger uppercase description, from NEARLY EVEN to EXTREME BIAS, animating a changed description while respecting reduced motion. ▲ indicates increased bias (lower entropy) after the latest roll, while ▼ indicates decreased bias (higher entropy). The arrows also show changes within a Tilt level; - indicates unchanged entropy, ignoring floating-point noise. Ordinary renders retain the indicator, while Shake, Jackpot, restart, and reload reset it to -. The tooltip and control’s accessible label describe the bias direction and whether the level changed. BOARD NET accumulates exact net income on the current die, persists across reloads, and resets on Shake, Jackpot replacement, or restart. Older saves initialize the new baseline without discarding progress or records. Settings links to the creator’s website, and About and all seven guide languages describe the current rules.

The title no longer includes the PLAY THE ODDS tagline, and the vertical gap between Number and ×6 is 2 px. The Number header owns the border above Odds, matching the neighboring column header’s bottom border above 5. Touch devices in the phone layout use a modal numeric keypad for bet amount edits, with no editable field that could invoke a native keyboard. Opening, typing, committing, and closing it leave the underlying board and controls at their existing coordinates and dimensions. The editor validates positive whole amounts, preserves integers beyond Number precision, and supports cancel, backspace, clear, and hardware keyboard entry. Its 10%, 20%, and 30% presets calculate from the current bankroll with exact integer arithmetic, rounding down to whole Gold and using a minimum of 1 G. Tests verify that draft bets do not reduce the preset's bankroll basis and that the presets follow settled bankroll changes. The saved mobile action switch reorders the actual Roll/Shake buttons and their portrait widths; landscape keeps equal widths, and desktop retains Roll first.
